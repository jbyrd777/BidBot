## Scope

Bid board (`src/app/bids/page.tsx`) and the screens that share its shell: bid detail and bot team. Mode: Operate. Build path: code led (Operate surface).

## Audience and task

Estimating leads triage every invitation to bid and make the bid or no bid call; sales checks what is due; owners glance on a phone. The task: see what is due, trust Bid Desk's first read, decide.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A flooring bid desk that feels like a native Apple app: calm, light, exact. It refuses the category default of a gray admin table with a blue button.

OWN-WORLD: Cool porcelain canvas washed with soft lime and sky color fields. Content sits on machined double bezel plates (frosted outer shell, white inner core with a top highlight). Glass is only the floating control layer: the island nav, the segmented filter lens, the decision panel. Lime is the single accent. Geist, one family, tabular figures. Phosphor icons at light weight.

STORY: The estimator sees how many bids wait on them and what is due when, reads Bid Desk's reason for each, and makes the call in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating glass island nav centered at top. Below, a bento row: a large lime plate with the board title and the waiting count, three compact stat plates that each filter the list. Then the three week due rail plate. The invitation list begins below the fold on a laptop.
